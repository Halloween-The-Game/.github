## 🧠 Overview

Halloween: The Game is a horror experience inspired by the iconic Halloween film franchise, featuring the infamous Michael Myers. Developed by IllFonic and Gun Interactive, the game brings players into a tense world of stalking, pursuit, and survival.

Its asymmetrical multiplayer format pits Michael Myers against a group of survivors, creating unpredictable encounters where stealth, awareness, and quick decisions are essential. The game also includes a single-player experience for those who prefer facing the horror alone.
